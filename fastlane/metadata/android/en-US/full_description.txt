<p><b>Suflyor</b> is a free, open-source teleprompter that listens to you. It floats over Instagram, TikTok or the camera app and scrolls the script as you speak, so you never chase the text.</p>

<p><b>Follows your voice</b></p>
<ul>
<li>On-device streaming speech recognition (sherpa-onnx). No cloud, no account.</li>
<li>Re-read a line and the text goes back with you. Skip a paragraph and it catches up.</li>
<li>Pauses and off-script talk leave the text where it is.</li>
</ul>

<p><b>Floats over any camera app</b></p>
<ul>
<li>Keeps listening while another app records video, and your video still gets the sound.</li>
<li>Text right under the front camera; lock mode lets touches reach the camera buttons.</li>
<li>Landscape mode, Quick Settings tile, volume keys and Bluetooth remotes.</li>
</ul>

<p><b>Imports almost anything</b>: TXT, Markdown, DOCX, ODT, RTF, HTML and PDF.</p>

<p><b>Private</b>: the app has no internet permission. Audio never leaves the phone. The accessibility service works only during a session and cannot read screen content.</p>

<p>Speaks English and Russian: the interface follows your phone's language, and the speech language is picked from your script.</p>
